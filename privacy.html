Tabletop Theory Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Tabletop Theory Publisher is a private tool used by the owner of the Tabletop Theory channel to upload the channel's own original videos to the owner's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, and to read those videos' performance statistics.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

  • Basic profile information of the owner's own account (e.g. display name, account ID), to confirm the connected account.
  • Permission to upload the owner's videos to that account.
  • Public performance statistics of videos the tool uploaded (views, likes, comments, shares).

How data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared or sent to any third party other than the platform's own official API.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in the platform's account settings and delete the locally stored tokens and statistics.

Contact

Questions: contact the Tabletop Theory channel owner through the channel's profile.
